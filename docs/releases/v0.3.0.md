A bugfix release for [Tekken 3 Expanded](https://github.com/omarma/tekken3-expanded) 1.5.0: the TTT1 characters' HD faces no longer cover the Jukebox's and Tournament Mode's screens, Tournament Mode's portraits and fights are right again, and the setup runs on non-English Windows. No game data is included: the setup makes everything from your own discs.

**Expanded 1.5.0 needs TMST 0.2.0 or later.** 0.3.0 is recommended for everyone on 0.2.0.

## Fixes

- **TTT1 icons covering the Jukebox and Tournament Mode** ([#2](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/2)): the Expanded's HD tiles and portraits of the TTT1 characters drew over the add-on's pictures (4:3 and widescreen). The pictures now leave the HD faces' places free, and Tournament Mode shows the TTT1 characters with those HD faces too.
- **Tournament Mode portraits switching on the bracket** ([#3](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/3)): the Tekken 3 fighters showed their small grid icon (or a wrong picture) on the bracket and next-match screens. Their big VS portraits are now taken from your Tekken 3 disc by the setup, and the ones the game keeps from a loading screen are kept again.
- **Tournament Mode black and silent fight** ([#5](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/5)): after a short loading screen the black cover stayed up for the whole fight (only the top of the screen showed when paused). The fight now always lifts it.
- **Setup stops at Install & play** ([#7](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/7)): on Windows in another language (e.g. German) the check whether the game is running failed, and the Tekken 1–2 music was never made. It now reads Windows' answer in the right code page.
- **Unknown's wolf behind other fighters**: after a fight against Unknown, a Tekken 3 fighter on its TTT1 model kept fighting with her wolf behind it. The wolf now appears only behind Unknown.

## New in the setup

- **Taking the Tekken 3 portraits:** a new step of the add-on's setup (it runs by itself with Update & play) takes Tournament Mode's portraits of the 21 Tekken 3 fighters from the Tekken 3 disc the Expanded runs (USA SLUS-00402). Another region or no disc: it is skipped, and each fighter has its grid icon until its first loading screen.

## Updating from 0.2.0

1. **Close the game.**
2. **Replace the add-on:**
   - with git: `git -C "<the Expanded's folder>/addons/tmst" pull`;
   - or from the ZIP: download `Source code (zip)` below, delete the old `addons/tmst` folder **but keep its `workspace` folder** (the music and stages the setup made; without it they are made again, which takes a few minutes), rename the unzipped `t3-expanded-addons-0.3.0` folder to `tmst`, put it in `addons`, and move the old `workspace` folder back into it. `addons/tmst/addon.cmake` must exist.
3. **Expanded 1.5.0 and later:** start the Expanded's setup and click **Update & play**. It sees the new version, runs the add-on's setup (the new portrait step included) and builds the game again. **1.4.1 and earlier:** run `addons/tmst/Setup TMST.cmd` (macOS: `.command`, Linux: `.sh`) and click **Install & play**.
4. **Start the game.** Your mod settings, Jukebox choices and tournament progress stay.

If the TMST group is missing in the Mods tab afterwards, `.setup/setup.log` tells why; run `Setup TMST` once more.
