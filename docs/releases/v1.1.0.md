TMST 1.1.0 for [Tekken 3 Expanded](https://github.com/omarma/tekken3-expanded) brings Tekken 1 Content (Tekken 1's 11 stages and its music from your own Tekken 1 disc), Tekken 2's Big Head Mode, a PERFORMANCE version of the Tekken 2 stages for slower computers, Unknown's intro and the player's costume for every opponent in Tournament Mode, the announcer's rounds, and the fixes since 1.0.0. It runs on Expanded 1.5.0 and 1.5.1. No game data is included: the setup makes everything from your own discs.

**Expanded 1.5.0 and later need TMST 0.2.0 or later.** 1.1.0 is recommended for everyone.

## New

- **Tekken 1 Content:** a new package in the TMST group, made from your Tekken 1 disc (USA SLUS-00006, Europe SCES-00005 or Japan SLPS-00040, given to `Setup TMST` in its own field), its parts in **TMST / Tekken 1 Content**.
  - **Stages:** Tekken 1's 11 stages, the sky and the layered backgrounds as large as Tekken 1 draws them, turning with the camera, and Tekken 1's floor tiles out to the horizon. **TEKKEN 1** is a game on the STAGE line (VS and Practice) and on GAME OPTION's **STAGES** row; RANDOM draws them too. As in Tekken 1: Monument Valley's sunset, King George Island's snow, the Stadium's big screen showing the fight. Each stage plays its own Tekken 1 theme, unless the opponent has a Jukebox piece.
  - **Music:** the Jukebox's Tekken 1 categories get the disc's CD audio (Venezia, Fighter Select, Staff Roll, Name Entry).
- **Big Head Mode (Tekken 2 Content):** Tekken 2's hidden mode on GAME OPTION's **TEKKEN 2 BIG HEAD MODE** row: **OFF**, **BIG** or **BIGGER**. The fighters' heads and hands grow, their limbs get thicker and their voices sound 3 semitones higher, TTT1 guests and Tekken Force's enemies included, in every mode. With Wireframe Mode on too, the camera stands back as far as the head has grown.
- **PERFORMANCE Tekken 2 stages** ([#13](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/13)): GAME OPTION's **TEKKEN 2 STAGES** row has a third setting after CLASSIC and REMAKE. It is the Remake with less on it, for slower computers: no grass, birds and other animals, fewer flying and falling things and less scenery; the backgrounds, the rain and the lightning stay. The Remake itself is unchanged.
- **Tournament Mode:**
  - **Unknown's intro:** before the Tekken Tag Tournament's Grand Final, Unknown kneels with her back to you, rises in her lightning and turns to you, her wolf burning behind her.
  - **The announcer** ([#20](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/20)): "Round 1", "Round 2", "Final Round" before FIGHT, as in Arcade.
  - **Costumes:** every opponent, the bosses too, wears the costume of the button you picked yours with (□ ✕ △ ○ R1), and the boss intros show the same costume. Xiaoyu and Jin in the school outfit fight at the school.
  - **Tekken 1 stages in the Tekken 1 Tournament** (with Tekken 1 Content's Stages on): a Tekken 1 stage for every match as in the original game, none twice, with its Tekken 1 theme.
  - The hints are drawn sharp.
- **Hong Kong Rooftop:** the floor's orange and teal lamps light the fighters from below, as in Tekken 2 (Classic and Remake).
- **The Jukebox's lists decide what is made:** the setup makes only the music the add-on's title lists name, so the pieces nobody plays take no room.

## Fixes

- **Tekken Force plays the Jukebox's music** ([#23](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/23)): the player's chosen piece in the stages, the boss's when the boss comes; without a choice, the game's own.
- **Mirror Darkness** ([#25](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/25)): no more trails collecting at the bottom of the screen; the fight's edges stay black as on Tekken 3's stages.
- **Big Head Mode's voices:** a TTT1 guest's voice follows the row when the same guest is picked again after a change (it kept the raised voice after OFF).

## Downloads

Each platform has its own ZIP below: `TMST-1.1.0-Windows.zip`, `TMST-1.1.0-macOS.zip`, `TMST-1.1.0-Linux.zip`.
Each holds one folder, `tmst`, ready to go into the Expanded's `addons` folder: no renaming. The Android APK is built
from the Windows or the macOS one.

## Updating from 1.0.0

1. **Close the game.**
2. **Replace the add-on:**
   - with git: `git -C "<the Expanded's folder>/addons/tmst" pull`;
   - from the ZIP: in `addons/tmst`, delete everything **except the `workspace` folder** (the music and stages the
     setup made; without it they are made again, which takes a few minutes), then extract your platform's ZIP into the
     Expanded's `addons` folder.
3. **Start the Expanded's setup and click Update & play.** It sees the new version, runs the add-on's setup and builds
   the game again. Only what is new is made: the PERFORMANCE Tekken 2 stages (from the Remake's) and the Jukebox's
   title lists; the music and the other stages stay as they are.
4. **For Tekken 1 Content:** run `addons/tmst/Setup TMST.cmd` (macOS: `.command`, Linux: `.sh`), give it your Tekken 1
   disc and click **Install & play**: it makes the Tekken 1 stages and music (about 115 MB of music). Then switch
   **Tekken 1 Content** on in the Mods tab.
5. **Start the game.** Your mod settings, Jukebox choices and tournament progress stay.

If the TMST group is missing in the Mods tab afterwards, `.setup/setup.log` tells why; run `Setup TMST` once more.

## Fresh install

Extract your platform's ZIP into the Expanded's `addons` folder, then follow the
[README's installation](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons#installation) for your platform.
