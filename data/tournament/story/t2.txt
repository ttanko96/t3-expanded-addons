TEKKEN 2

Two years have passed since Kazuya Mishima won the first “King of the Iron Fists” tournament. With the mysterious disappearance of Heihachi Mishima, Kazuya Mishima – the new head of the Mishima Zaibatsu organization – is holding the tournament a second time. Once again, the best fighters from around the world have gathered to compete for the coveted title “The King of the Iron Fists.” There is a rumor that Kazuya has made a deal with the Devil. So enter the tournament at your own risk!
